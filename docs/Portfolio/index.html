About This Portfolio

Hello!

I'm darlak97, I studied Computer Systems Engineering and have been creating mods since 2020.
I am passionate about creating content and events for communities, and I've been studying and practicing various aspects of content creation. My main has been programming, which has allowed me to develop mods for different games, including Don't Starve Together, Arma 3, and Project Zomboid.
To create different kinds of content, I've also explored drawing, 3D modeling, and animation.

I've wanted to be part of Hytale's development since it was announced in 2018. After some time, I decided to start creating content through mods and collaborating on projects with development teams, hoping to eventually become part of the team. I was saddened when the game's cancellation was announced, but the #SaveHytale gave me even more motivation and renewed my hope of becoming part of this incredible game!

I hope you enjoy my portfolio. I'll continue sharing new content every day!
